The method

Seven gates, and three that exist only to void the other seven.

A concept enters the ledger only if it clears all seven: extensively proven in the literature; almost non-existent in the open market; capable of large unit margins; startable on very low capital; fast to produce; addressing a market big enough to matter; and measurably outperforming the product the buyer already pays for.

That last gate does the killing. It is not enough to be cheaper, greener, biodegradable or novel — those are preferences, not performance. The concept has to beat a named incumbent, on the metric that buyer already optimises for, at the incumbent's own price, by a step change of at least two-fold.

Then three sub-tests try to void the result. Is the named comparator actually what the buyer purchases today — or is it a withdrawn product, or a rival lab technique that never reached market? Is the comparison like-for-like — a finished product against a finished product, not a raw material against a formulated one? And how many months and how much capital until the first paid delivery, counting every certification and approval?

Finally the economics are recomputed from scratch. The report supplies only sourceable inputs — feedstock cost, conversion yield, the incumbent's price, an itemised equipment list, cycle time. Margin, breakeven, payback, capital productivity and return are calculated in code, then stress-tested against price falling, yield falling and capital doubling. We never ask the research model for a rate of return: a single confident number hides a dozen invented assumptions.

The graveyard

27 concepts failed. Here is exactly how.

No other research service will show you this, because showing it means admitting most ideas do not survive contact with evidence. The rejections are the most useful thing we produce: each one is a trap you no longer have to fall into.

Voided — dead incumbent

Biomanufactured magnetosomes for MRI contrast

Genuinely 2.4–3.5× the relaxivity of its named comparator. That comparator was Feridex — withdrawn from the market in 2008. The margin model was anchored to the historical price of a product discontinued for lack of demand. Regulatory path priced at $35M and 84 months.

Voided — straw incumbent

Plant-urease enzyme-induced carbonate precipitation

Benchmarked against MICP, a rival laboratory technique that never reached market. Against the real incumbent — Portland cement and soil-cement at 1.5–8 MPa — its 6.41 MPa sits inside the ordinary range. Beating another lab proves nothing.

Voided — mismatched comparison

Enzymatic estolide biolubricants

Claimed a 5.6× wear advantage. The baseline turned out to be diesel fuel, not a formulated lubricant — the EN 590 fuel spec caps at 460 µm. Corrected like-for-like: ~1.9×. Recomputed honestly: 68.5% gross margin but 31.7-month payback and a 4.9% annual return, negative under any adverse assumption.

Voided — negative control

AHPND-targeting bacteriophage for shrimp

Survival of 78.5–90.9% against ~45% — but the ~45% bundled antibiotics together with "no treatment". Beating an untreated pond shows the product does something, not that it beats what the farmer buys. The delta also fell below the 2× bar.

Voided — mismatched comparison

Marine-Safe Biomimetic Sunscreen Filters via Ultrasound-Assisted Extraction of Mycosporine-Like Amino Acids

The concept fails Sub-test B (Like-for-Like) because the claimed performance advantage relies on the molar extinction coefficient of pure isolated MAAs (Porphyra-334/Shinorine), whereas the commercial product being manufactured is a 17.9% semi-purified crude biomass extract. Additionally, by registering as a cosmetic extract (INCI) to avoid FDA OTC drug approval, it cannot legally perform the primary regulated UV-filtering function of Oxybenzone. Named comparator: Standard Organic UV Filters (Oxybenzone).

Voided — comparison and regulatory path

Enteric Drug Delivery Vehicles via Phosphoric Acidolysis of Natural Plant Spores

The concept fails low CapEx and velocity criteria due to novel excipient regulatory requirements, clinical trials, and Drug Master File filings exceeding 18 months and $5M. Furthermore, it fails like-for-like evaluation because the 249.9-fold bioavailability claim compares an enteric polymer-coated SEC against an uncoated oral drug baseline rather than a finished commercial enteric-coated formulation. Named comparator: Synthetic PLGA Microspheres / Uncoated Oral Delivery.

Voided — illegitimate incumbent

High-Yield, Premium Hydrocolloids via Synergistic Ultrasound-Microwave-Assisted Extraction (UMAE) of Citrus Biowaste

The concept fails incumbent legitimacy because the 2.45-fold yield superiority claim is calculated against Microwave-Assisted Extraction (MAE), a non-commercial academic lab technique, rather than the entrenched commercial industry standard of hot acid extraction. Additionally, extraction yield is an internal manufacturing efficiency metric rather than the primary product performance metric evaluated by hydrocolloid buyers. Named comparator: Microwave-Assisted Extraction (MAE) / Conventional Hot Acid Extraction.

Voided — mismatched comparison

Recombinant Ice-Binding Proteins (IBPs) via Methylotrophic Yeast Fermentation

The concept fails like-for-like form factor by comparing an unformulated active protein additive against a bulk solvent CPA (DMSO) or an untreated dough control. Furthermore, IBPs only partially replace cryoprotectant functions (preventing recrystallization rather than colligative ice suppression) and the report lacks a quantified head-to-head numerical viability percentage against DMSO. Named comparator: DMSO / Glycerol CPAs.

Voided — mismatched comparison

Biochar-Supported Sulfidated Nanoscale Zero-Valent Iron (S-nZVI@BC) for Rapid TCE Groundwater Remediation

The venture fails like-for-like form factor by comparing lab chemical degradation kinetics (Kobs) of an in-situ material composite directly against the multi-decade operating timeline of an ex-situ mechanical pumping plant. In addition, site-specific hydrogeologic permitting and field injection validation delay first paid delivery well beyond 18 months. Named comparator: Pump-and-Treat Systems (with Activated Carbon).

Voided — mismatched comparison

Halophile-Derived Siderophores via Non-Sterile Hypersaline Fermentation

Compares unformulated filtered fermentation broth directly against a fully formulated commercial Fe-EDDHA chelate product, violating Sub-test B (Like-for-Like). Additionally, the report fails to provide cited quantitative head-to-head field or plant performance data against Fe-EDDHA. Named comparator: Synthetic Fe-EDDHA (e.g., Sprint 138).

Why this is not a trend newsletter

Trend tools find what is already crowded.

Trend and case-study services

Signal source: the market itself

  • Detect rising search volume — by definition the thing is already visible, and already being acted on.
  • Document businesses that already earn revenue — so the opportunity is to copy someone with a head start.
  • The value decays as the subscriber list grows: a niche shared with 50,000 readers is no longer a niche.
  • No mechanism exists to reject an idea. More ideas looks like more value.

The Absence Audit

Signal source: the peer-reviewed record

  • Detects processes proven in the literature and absent from the market — there is no trend to be late to.
  • Documents businesses that do not exist yet, with the mechanism and operating conditions to build one.
  • The value does not decay with list size: the barrier is capability, not awareness. Knowing about a process does not let you run it.
  • Rejects roughly four out of five candidates, and publishes the reason for every rejection.

One tells you what is working. The other tells you what is proven, unbuilt, and why nobody has built it.

What arrives

Four files per concept, not a paragraph.

Every claim traces to a resolved citation. Non-peer-reviewed sources are tagged as such — repositories, theses and patents are labelled so you can weigh them yourself.

report.md

The full analysis

Mechanism, operating protocol with real conditions and yields, techno-economics, comparison against alternatives, the superiority table, and the absence audit explaining why it is not already on sale.

economics.json

The raw primitives

Feedstock cost, conversion yield, incumbent price, itemised capital, cycle time — each attached to its citation. You can recompute every ratio yourself, or change an assumption and watch it break.

economics.md

Computed unit economics

COGS, gross margin at the incumbent's price, contribution, breakeven volume, payback, capital productivity and return — plus a six-scenario stress test for price, yield, feedstock and capital.

audit.md

The red-team verdict

An independent pass that checks the comparator and the arithmetic rather than the prose, and overrules the scoring model when they disagree. It has done so on 8 of 33 concepts so far.
