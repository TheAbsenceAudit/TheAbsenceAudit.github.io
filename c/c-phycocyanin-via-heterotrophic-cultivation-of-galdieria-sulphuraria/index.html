C-Phycocyanin via Heterotrophic Cultivation of Galdieria sulphuraria

Rejected

This concept did not clear the framework. The reasoning is published in full below.

Why it failed

  • Voided by: Capital and time to revenue, D

Assessment

The concept fails venture criteria due to severely negative gross margins, excessive CapEx ($1.25M), and extended regulatory delays (48 months to revenue). Heterotrophic carbon consumption leads to an OpEx of $405/kg against an incumbent market price of $130/kg. Technical stability improvements cannot overcome these broken unit economics.

Claimed advantage

Maintains stability down to pH 2.8 and up to 60°C, whereas Spirulina E25 denatures above 45°C and irreversibly precipitates below pH 4.0.

Named comparator: Spirulina Extract (C-Phycocyanin, E25 Grade)

A rejection is not a claim that the science is wrong. It means the venture case did not survive: the comparator was not what the buyer actually buys, the comparison was not like-for-like, the advantage fell short of a step change, or the capital and time to first revenue were prohibitive.
