Enzymatic Synthesis of Estolide Biolubricants via Direct Castor Oil Transesterification

Rejected

This concept did not clear the framework. The reasoning is published in full below.

Why it failed

  • Voided by: Mismatched comparison

Assessment

The concept fails sub-test B because the wear scar comparison (~600 µm baseline) relies on diesel fuel lubricity standards rather than a like-for-like fully formulated lubricant benchmark. Furthermore, mandatory OEM pump testing and REACH/VGP environmental certifications push time to first paid delivery beyond 18 months.

Claimed advantage

Wear scar diameter of 106 µm vs ~600 µm (standard diesel benchmark) / ~200 µm (PAO)

Named comparator: Polyalphaolefin (PAO / PAO 4 / PAO 6) and Mineral Oil

Also flagged

  • Evidence rests on non-peer-reviewed sources
  • Single-source evidence base
  • no_production_runsheet

A rejection is not a claim that the science is wrong. It means the venture case did not survive: the comparator was not what the buyer actually buys, the comparison was not like-for-like, the advantage fell short of a step change, or the capital and time to first revenue were prohibitive.
