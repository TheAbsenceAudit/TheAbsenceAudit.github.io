Nitrogen-Fixing Cyanobacterial Biocrust Consortia via Thin-Film Cellulose Matrix Propagation

Rejected

This concept did not clear the framework. The reasoning is published in full below.

Why it failed

  • No head-to-head evidence

Assessment

While biological nitrogen fixation and biocrust formation are well-documented, phototrophic cyanobacterial film propagation in greenhouses suffers from slow growth kinetics compared to heterotrophic fermentations. Furthermore, supplying broad-acre agriculture requires massive greenhouse surface area, limiting rapid mass adoption scalability.

Also flagged
