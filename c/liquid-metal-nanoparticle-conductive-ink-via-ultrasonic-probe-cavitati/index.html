Liquid Metal Nanoparticle Conductive Ink via Ultrasonic Probe Cavitation

Rejected

This concept did not clear the framework. The reasoning is published in full below.

Why it failed

Assessment

Leverages top-down ultrasonic cavitation of commodity EGaIn alloy to replace brittle silver inks. Achieves >50x superior resistance stability under dynamic cyclic strain at price parity, with $35,000 startup CapEx and an 82% gross margin.

Claimed advantage

Resistance change after 1,000 to 5,000 stretching cycles: Silver Flake Ink >1,000% increase vs Liquid Metal Ink <19.23% increase (>50x superior)

Named comparator: Silver flake ink

Also flagged

  • Price parity asserted, not sourced
  • unsourced_equipment

A rejection is not a claim that the science is wrong. It means the venture case did not survive: the comparator was not what the buyer actually buys, the comparison was not like-for-like, the advantage fell short of a step change, or the capital and time to first revenue were prohibitive.
