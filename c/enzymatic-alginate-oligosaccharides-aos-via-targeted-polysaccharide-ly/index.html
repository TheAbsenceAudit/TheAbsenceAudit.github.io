Enzymatic Alginate Oligosaccharides (AOS) via Targeted Polysaccharide Lyase Depolymerization

Rejected

This concept did not clear the framework. The reasoning is published in full below.

Why it failed

  • Voided by: D, Negative control / sub-2x, Form-factor / handling regression
  • Below the 2x step-change bar

Assessment

The venture fails multiple key criteria, including gross margin (56.1% vs required 70%), initial CapEx ($265,000 vs $250,000 limit), and superiority delta (~0.97x to 1.1x vs required >2.0x). High input costs of refined sodium alginate and form factor regression further invalidate the concept.

Claimed advantage

Root biomass increase of ~0.97x to 1.1x relative to Ascophyllum nodosum extract (+40.0% AOS vs +41.1% ANE in wheat; +20.2% AOS vs +18% ANE in tomato/soybean)

Named comparator: Acadian Plant Health Ascophyllum nodosum extract (AscoStar / Stella Maris)

Also flagged

  • Price parity asserted, not sourced
  • unsourced_equipment
  • no_opex

A rejection is not a claim that the science is wrong. It means the venture case did not survive: the comparator was not what the buyer actually buys, the comparison was not like-for-like, the advantage fell short of a step change, or the capital and time to first revenue were prohibitive.
