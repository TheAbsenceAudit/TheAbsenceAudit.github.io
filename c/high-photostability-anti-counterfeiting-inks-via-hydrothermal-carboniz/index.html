High-Photostability Anti-Counterfeiting Inks via Hydrothermal Carbonization of Biomass into Carbon Quantum Dots

Rejected

This concept did not clear the framework. The reasoning is published in full below.

Why it failed

  • Voided by: D, Form-factor / handling regression
  • opex_driven_margin

Assessment

The venture explicitly concedes failing the 70% gross margin threshold, reaching only 38.9% due to incumbent wholesale pricing ($45/kg) and nitrogen dopant costs. Additionally, the unpreserved aqueous formulation suffers a handling and shelf-life regression compared to commercially stabilized incumbent ink cartridges.

Claimed advantage

Retains >90% signal after 55 hours continuous UV exposure versus incumbent losing >70% signal in a few hours (>10x operational photostability improvement)

Named comparator: Shenzhen Goosam UV Fluorescent Water-Based Invisible Ink (L3258/L3151 series)

Also flagged

  • Price parity asserted, not sourced

A rejection is not a claim that the science is wrong. It means the venture case did not survive: the comparator was not what the buyer actually buys, the comparison was not like-for-like, the advantage fell short of a step change, or the capital and time to first revenue were prohibitive.
