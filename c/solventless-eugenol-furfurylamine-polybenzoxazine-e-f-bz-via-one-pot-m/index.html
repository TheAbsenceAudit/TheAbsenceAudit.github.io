Why it failed

  • Self-declared operational arbitrage (no patentable asset)

Assessment

The proposal demonstrates high scientific rigor and commercial feasibility by translating a published bio-based polybenzoxazine synthesis into aerospace secondary composite structures. Unit economics show a 73.4% gross margin at price parity ($95/kg) with $124,000 startup CapEx and 9 months to first revenue. All gating criteria and voiding sub-tests are fully satisfied.

Also flagged

  • Price parity asserted, not sourced
  • Weak hook — known class characteristic / unverified superlative
  • No patent prior art identified (FTO unverified)
  • No comparative data matrix
  • No claim hierarchy

A rejection is not a claim that the science is wrong. It means the venture case did not survive: the comparator was not what the buyer actually buys, the comparison was not like-for-like, the advantage fell short of a step change, or the capital and time to first revenue were prohibitive.
