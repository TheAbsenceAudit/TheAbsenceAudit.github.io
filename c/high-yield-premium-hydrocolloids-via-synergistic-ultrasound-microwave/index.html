High-Yield, Premium Hydrocolloids via Synergistic Ultrasound-Microwave-Assisted Extraction (UMAE) of Citrus Biowaste

Rejected

This concept did not clear the framework. The reasoning is published in full below.

Why it failed

  • Voided by: Illegitimate incumbent

Assessment

The concept fails incumbent legitimacy because the 2.45-fold yield superiority claim is calculated against Microwave-Assisted Extraction (MAE), a non-commercial academic lab technique, rather than the entrenched commercial industry standard of hot acid extraction. Additionally, extraction yield is an internal manufacturing efficiency metric rather than the primary product performance metric evaluated by hydrocolloid buyers.

Claimed advantage

2.45-fold increase in pectin yield (65.40% vs 26.63%) compared to non-commercial MAE

Named comparator: Microwave-Assisted Extraction (MAE) / Conventional Hot Acid Extraction

Also flagged

  • Single-source evidence base
  • no_production_runsheet

A rejection is not a claim that the science is wrong. It means the venture case did not survive: the comparator was not what the buyer actually buys, the comparison was not like-for-like, the advantage fell short of a step change, or the capital and time to first revenue were prohibitive.
