The metabolism

One loop. Nothing wasted. Nothing bolted on.

The system is drawn as a single organism. Energy flows in once and is collected again at every point it would otherwise escape — inside the system, not beside it.

  1. The sun enters

    Utility solar at the proven IPP route — built inside its bankable region, the numbers already settled in the design.

  2. Water rises once — and pays rent coming down

    Desalination with isobaric energy recovery on the brine, brackish-water plants inland, reuse at 30%+; the return legs run pump-as-turbines, so what gravity was paid to lift, gravity gives back.

  3. The acid stream becomes the treasury

    The phosphoric stream yields uranium and heavy rare earths; the phosphogypsum cures into fire-rated board under its own CO&sub2; — the liability monetized inside the same pipework.

  4. Heat never leaves the loop

    The acid plant’s exotherm and the kilns preheat desalination feed and greenhouses; the datacenters’ reject heat warms the same circuits — cold sea in, warm district out.

  5. Brine is a product

    Salt, magnesium and minerals recovered from every brine stream — disposal becomes production.

  6. The desert feeds without thirst

    Terfas in full sun (2,000–3,000 ha, a third reserved for locals ≤€12/kg), spirulina ponds, greenhouses — the chain that survives the drought is the chain we build.

  7. Health is the first output

    Point-of-use filters and clean air — $2,592 per healthy year, the best investment in the economy — bought with the ESG concession the loop itself earns.

  8. Money closes the circle

    Compute · metals · protein feed hard currency → reserves → rating → refinancing the trap → reinvestment. The sovereign money cycle, every variable on the table.

This is how a dry country is saved: not one giant canal, but a mesh of three water tiers carried on pipelines, powered by the loop’s own solar, paid by the loop’s own yields. Dams sit at record lows and much of the groundwater is brine — so the plan pipes water where the arithmetic lets it travel, and recovers energy on every leg it descends.

Filters, at the tap

Before any pipe is laid, the cheapest cut: point-of-use filters and clean-air boxes in homes and schools — $2,592 per healthy year, the best investment in the economy, nothing piped, no permits, live in 90 days.

Tier 1 — months to years

Coastal desal for the cities · brackish for the basin

150,000 m³/d coastal SWRO serves the coast at $0.35/m³ integrated — 28% of SONEDE’s own produced cost, every credit from inside the loop’s pipework. Inland, the basin runs on brackish-water RO at ~$0.34/m³ — inside SONEDE’s own brackish band ($0.10–0.70/m³, Pg16) — and reuse at 30%+ turns every drop twice. The pumping physics is priced: 1.09 kWh/m³ per 400 m lift is the floor that decides where water may travel[Rw4].

The water pays rent coming down

Every leg the water descends, the loop collects: return flows run pump-as-turbines (3.9 GWh/yr — 70% of 150,000 m³/d at 50 m head), brine pressure feeds back through the ERD, and the same mesh shifts its pumping with the solar curve (20 GWh/yr of dispatch value). What gravity was paid to lift, gravity gives back — the water system is also the loop’s battery, and its bill lands at $0.35/m³ against the $4.90B fuel and $3.00B food import lines the same mesh relieves.

The monetary underlying

The Fed’s rate cycle is the weather this state lives under — the loop is the roof

No sovereign design in a dollar-denominated frontier economy is honest without naming the transmission from Washington. Every figure in this section is measured — pulled from the World Bank API on 2026-09-09 — not restated from memory[Pg1].

Channel 1 — the debt trap

Original sin, compounded by the Fed

Tunisia cannot borrow long-term in its own currency abroad — the “original sin” of every emerging state[Pg12]. External debt stands at $40.46B, 78.7% of GDP, a large share dollar-denominated. When the Fed raises rates, global yields climb in tandem and refinancing turns expensive; debt service already absorbs 26.2% of export revenues and drains the reserves (9.34B = 4.3 months of imports, World Bank 2024; Fitch’s anchor is ~4.5 months[Rc11]). US monetary conditions are the anchor of the whole emerging-market cycle — the “dilemma, not trilemma”[Pg13]: a small state with a dollar trap does not set its own weather.

Channel 2 — imported inflation

The orthodoxy trap: hold rates, strangle growth

A wide interest-rate differential pulls capital toward US safe assets, pressing the dinar down — which immediately worsens imported inflation, because Tunisia is a net importer of hydrocarbons and cereals priced in dollars: food and fuel imports are $3.00B + $4.90B a year (11.5% + 18.8% of merchandise imports, World Bank 2024). The BCT — autonomous and non-monetizing under Organic Law 2016-35[Pg4][Pg5] — must hold its own rates up to keep dinar placements attractive. That orthodoxy is correct and asphyxiating: credit freezes, investment stalls, growth stays below 2.5% with inflation at 5.2% (2025). The dead end is arithmetic, not policy failure.

The loop’s answer: earn the weather, stop renting it

The program does not fight the Fed — it decouples the transmission. The three owned-export engines — compute · strategic metals · protein — earn ~$117M/yr of gross exports outside the Fed cycle, and the import-substitution legs (solar displacement + food) take $152M/yr out of the dollar invoice directly — the same $3B food and $5B fuel import lines the Fed’s second channel punishes. Reserves rise, the rating path runs B-/Caa1 → BB-, and the refinancing premium the Fed sets shrinks for the state that has its own hard-currency engines. Monetary orthodoxy stays exactly where it belongs — at the BCT — while the real economy stops renting dollars and starts producing them. That is the sovereign answer to the original sin: not defiance of the Fed, but independence from the channel.

The equilibrium

Energy and money, balanced by design

Nash’s standard: a state is an equilibrium when nothing can improve it by moving alone. Here every module is cash-positive on its own, and the whole is net-positive because nothing leaves the loop.

Ledger A — energy

The system recovers inside itself

  • Desalination ERD: isobaric ERD already inside the 4 kWh/m3 SWRO benchmark (~35-40% of RO energy); adding it on top would double-count — carried as zero here, not added twice.

  • Balance: ≈33.9 GWh/yr recovered + 20 GWh/yr dispatch value — every leg computed from cited primitives, in the audit below.

The two futures

Darwinian evolution — with the loop, and without it

The selection machine is already running. The loop is the intervention that flips which strategy wins.

Future A — the default

Without the loop

Dose accumulates: fluoride in the teeth before age 8, cadmium in the kidneys for decades, dust in the lungs. The fittest emigrate — engineer to Paris, founder to London; InstaDeep’s $680M exit is the emblem. Every departure makes the basin harder for those who stay. The nation evolves into a smaller, older, sicker version of itself that exports its children and imports its food. That future requires no decision.

Future B — the plan

With the loop

We change the environment, not the genes: clean water → intact enamel and strong bones within one generation; protein → nourished brains; jobs → a reason to stay; exports → room to breathe. Same people, same geography — a different fitness landscape. The plan is evolution by other means.

This is not a pilot in a greenhouse. The first asset

The nursery — not the field

6,000 seedlings per hectare means 18M seedlings for the ceiling — so the nursery, not the land, is the binding constraint. The plan builds 5M seedlings/yr of local-ecotype capacity first — wild Tunisian terfas genetics, adapted to the basin’s own soil — and the hectares follow the nursery, not the other way around. This is also why the Saudi failure[Rc17] is not our risk: they imported soil biology; we propagate the truffle that already grows here.

The jobs

Hectares are employment

At 2,500 ha: ~200 nursery workers (5M seedlings/yr ÷ 25,000 per worker-year), ~1,000 field workers (one per 2.5 ha of maintenance), ~783 seasonal collectors (940 t ÷ 20 kg per collector-day ÷ 60-day season) — plus transport, sorting and the domestic-quota market stalls. In the towns where the blockades began, the truffle is the first harvest that does not need the state’s permission to be valuable.

The honest cap — and what the thousands of hectares do NOT carry

Terfas is a luxury: the market absorbs 1,000–1,500 t/yr, which is why the plan stops at ~3,000 ha. The mass protein and vegetables for the people come from the other rungs of the food stack — 0.7 ha of spirulina feeds the basin’s children at the tested dose, and the greenhouses do volume. Terfas is the arid-land cash crop: it converts hectares nothing else can farm into euros, FX and seasonal wages — and its first 100 ha must pass the 3-season gate before one hectare more is planted. The ambition is thousands of hectares; the discipline is the gate.

The objection, answered

If the arithmetic works, why not begin tomorrow?

Because a design of this weight lives or dies on measurements that cost almost nothing — and the moment those numbers land, the sequence above is the order of work.

Ports and ships move goods across a sea. This system moves energy, water, food and money without one. The arithmetic is net-positive on both ledgers, and it was not reached by faith: every figure on this page is computed in code from sourced primitives, and every check the arithmetic demanded is already built into the design you are reading. What separates this page from the first spade in the ground is therefore not capital, and not consensus — it is ninety days of measurements that no honest engineer skips: the water chemistry, the acid-stream assays, the emissions inventory. Phase 0 costs $150–250k, builds nothing, and converts this design into signed numbers. The day those numbers land, the first act is the filters — protecting the people who will build everything that follows.

The uranium bridge — the 716,500 lbs, derived

The metals volume is derived from cited primitives: GCT’s 6.5 Mt/yr rock throughput (Rm17) × 45–140 ppm uranium in Gafsa rock (Ru5) × 88% partitioning to the wet-process acid (Ru1) × 62% recovery → 412–1,283k lbs U&sb3;O&sb8;/yr; the page’s 716.5k sits inside the band, and the process itself is public, expired-patent technology (Pg19) operated at industrial scale for two decades (Ru1). The Phase 0 assay converts the band into a measured stream concentration — the recovery gate (≥85%, ≤$75/lb) then binds the economics.

Where the water gate breaks — and why it holds

the gate sits between SONEDE's own $1.00-1.50/m3 (4k-60k m3/d) and the mega-plant class $0.40-0.60 — it is only achievable at the full 150,000 m3/d scale on solar power; built small, the leg fails against the utility's own numbers, which is exactly why the gate exists The same SONEDE deck prices brackish water at $0.10–0.70/m³ — which is exactly why the inland basin runs on brackish water.

The structure

Six modules, one metabolism

The design is six independently bankable modules sharing power, water, data and workforce. The loop is the outcome, never the precondition — no module’s economics depends on another module succeeding, and every module’s gate is public. Each one that passes scales; each one that fails retires alone — the arithmetic decides, in public.
