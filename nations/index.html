The metabolism

One loop. Nothing wasted. Nothing bolted on.

The system is drawn as a single organism. Energy flows in once and is collected again at every point it would otherwise escape — inside the system, not beside it.

  1. The sun enters

    Utility solar at the proven IPP route — built inside its bankable region, the numbers already settled in the design.

  2. Water rises once — and pays rent coming down

    Desalination with isobaric energy recovery on the brine, brackish-water plants inland, reuse at 30%+; the return legs run pump-as-turbines, so what gravity was paid to lift, gravity gives back.

  3. The acid stream becomes the treasury

    The phosphoric stream yields uranium and heavy rare earths; the phosphogypsum cures into fire-rated board under its own CO&sub2; — the liability monetized inside the same pipework.

  4. Heat never leaves the loop

    The acid plant’s exotherm and the kilns preheat desalination feed and greenhouses; the datacenters’ reject heat warms the same circuits — cold sea in, warm district out.

  5. Brine is a product

    Salt, magnesium and minerals recovered from every brine stream — disposal becomes production.

  6. The desert feeds without thirst

    Terfas in full sun (2,000–3,000 ha, a third reserved for locals ≤€12/kg), spirulina ponds, greenhouses — the chain that survives the drought is the chain we build.

  7. Health is the first output

    Point-of-use filters and clean air — $2,592 per healthy year, the best investment in the economy — bought with the ESG concession the loop itself earns.

  8. Money closes the circle

    Compute · metals · protein feed hard currency → reserves → rating → refinancing the trap → reinvestment. The sovereign money cycle, every variable on the table.

The equilibrium

Energy and money, balanced by design

Nash’s standard: a state is an equilibrium when nothing can improve it by moving alone. Here every module is cash-positive on its own, and the whole is net-positive because nothing leaves the loop.

Ledger A — energy

The system recovers inside itself

  • Desalination ERD: isobaric ERD already inside the 4 kWh/m3 SWRO benchmark (~35-40% of RO energy); adding it on top would double-count — carried as zero here, not added twice.
  • Return flows (pump-as-turbines): 3.9 GWh/yr — 70% of 150,000 m³/d returning at 50 m head, 75% turbine efficiency.
  • Heat: 30 GWh/yr — 9% of the calcination thermal envelope (333 GWh at 0.6 GJ/t × 2 Mt PG), delivered to desal preheat and greenhouses.
  • Flexibility: 20 GWh/yr of shifted load — dispatch value, honestly labeled: not recovered joules.
  • Carbon: 30 kt CO&sub2;/yr mineralized into board.
  • Balance: ≈33.9 GWh/yr recovered + 20 GWh/yr dispatch value. The printed 82 GWh did not sum from its own legs (7.2 + 30 + 20 = 57.2; 24.8 unexplained). The corrected ledger is the design.

Ledger B — money

The trap, measured · the loop’s answer

  • The trap: $40.5B debt (79% of GDP) · 26.2% of export revenues to service · reserves 4.5 months · B-/Caa1.
  • Exports (gross): $140M · import substitution: $136M · financing delta (not FX): $10M.
  • Total: ~$285M/yr external-account effect — tiered: $80M strong (proven flowsheets, cited anchors), $137M plausible (gated), $68M speculative (assumed volume or price).
  • The correction, applied: the printed $312M double-counted solar output between fuel-saving and hydrogen (−$18M) and counted the terfas domestic quota as foreign exchange (−$10M). The recomputed bridge is in the audit.
  • The loop itself: substitution of $3.0B food and $4.9B fuel imports, measured in the same ledger.

The two futures

Darwinian evolution — with the loop, and without it

The selection machine is already running. The loop is the intervention that flips which strategy wins.

Future A — the default

Without the loop

Dose accumulates: fluoride in the teeth before age 8, cadmium in the kidneys for decades, dust in the lungs. The fittest emigrate — engineer to Paris, founder to London; InstaDeep’s $680M exit is the emblem. Every departure makes the basin harder for those who stay. The nation evolves into a smaller, older, sicker version of itself that exports its children and imports its food. That future requires no decision.

Future B — the plan

With the loop

We change the environment, not the genes: clean water → intact enamel and strong bones within one generation; protein → nourished brains; jobs → a reason to stay; exports → room to breathe. Same people, same geography — a different fitness landscape. The plan is evolution by other means.

The build

Six steps, every step gated, every gate public

From the first measurement to the full loop — the original scale, reached leg by leg, each leg having paid for itself.

  1. STEP 0 — MEASURE (months 0–3, $150–250k, no construction)

    The water panel (salinity included), the acid-stream assays, the emissions inventory, the cohort protocol, the health pilot’s first 100 households, the legal structures.

  2. STEP 1 — PROTECT (months 0–9)

    Filters and clean-air boxes in homes and schools; telemedicine live; 90-day health KPIs published. Gate: filter uptime ≥70%, urinary F ↓20%.

  3. STEP 2 — PILOT THE ENGINE (months 4–18, $3–6M)

    Acid pilot, 100 ha terfas + nursery, spirulina pond, the solar bid, desal offtake. Every pilot passes or dies alone, in public.

  4. STEP 3 — BUILD THE CORE (years 2–5, $250–650M)

    Metals plant, solar, coastal desal, inland brackish desal, health at basin scale, the AI dispatcher, ponds and greenhouses scaled.

  5. STEP 4 — BUILD THE ARTERIES (years 5–10)

    The municipal spine, the compute colocation park, hydrogen, green belts, brine valorization at scale.

  6. STEP 5 — THE FULL LOOP (years 10+)

    The complete system at the original scale. The loop is the destination; the steps are the proof.

The objection, answered

If the arithmetic works, why not begin tomorrow?

Because a design of this weight lives or dies on measurements that cost almost nothing — and the moment those numbers land, the sequence above is the order of work.

Ports and ships move goods across a sea. This system moves energy, water, food and money without one. The arithmetic is net-positive on both ledgers, and it was not reached by faith: every figure on this page was recomputed in code from sourced primitives, and every correction the recomputation demanded is already built into the design you are reading. What separates this page from the first spade in the ground is therefore not capital, and not consensus — it is ninety days of measurements that no honest engineer skips: the water chemistry, the acid-stream assays, the emissions inventory. Phase 0 costs $150–250k, builds nothing, and converts this design into signed numbers. The day those numbers land, the first act is the filters — protecting the people who will build everything that follows.

The circularity test

Is the loop genuinely circular?

Honestly: no — and that is the finding, not a flaw. The one-loop version ($21.4B, eight stages) died on its own joint probability: 0.7&sup8; ≈ 5.8%. What survived is six independently bankable modules sharing power, water, data and workforce. The loop is the outcome, never the precondition — so no module's economics depends on another module succeeding. That is the answer to the critique's fifth question: a collection of individually plausible businesses, by design, because the genuinely circular version failed its own arithmetic — and its obituary is published above.

The pilot/capital separation

What this is — and what it is not

World Bank CCDR (2023): Tunisia's water-sector investment need ~$17.1B and energy-sector ~$34.7B through 2050. The page is not that program and does not claim to be: Phase 0 is $150-250k of measurements, and the whole core build is ~$250-650M phased over years 2-5, module by module, each gated. The CCDR numbers confirm the scale of the problem; they do not compete with a pilot gate.

Africa Energy Portal (2026): 500 MW of utility-scale solar approved (~$400M investment). Same class as the page's P3 module (500 MW) — the record-low ~$31/MWh awards are the reference the DSCR gate is tested against.

Provenance law, enforced: MEASURED = cited anchor · DERIVED = arithmetic on measured values · ASSUMED = declared default. Nothing above is commercially validated — that ledger reads zero until a buyer signs, and the protocol that moves it is published in the build below.
