How the pipeline runs

  • Two research scans a day— the engine reads the peer-reviewed record for demonstrated processes and drafts an assessment per candidate with works cited.
  • Thirteen automated checks— every candidate passes the seven gates, the three void tests, the economics recomputed in code, the independent red-team audit, the IP critique and the patent-citation check.
  • Two publishes a day— the ledger, this page and the track record are regenerated from the corpus at every publish, so a number on the site is never older than the last scan.
  • Fail closed— any gate failure blocks the publish entirely; the pipeline cannot silently ship a wrong number.

Priority window

New dossiers are released to subscribers7 days before their public release date. The public sees the concept, its verdict and its date of release; subscribers open the dossier immediately. It is the difference between reading a newsletter and buying deal flow: the pursuit window opens before the crowd. No dossier currently sits inside the window; the next one that clears the gates enters it automatically.
